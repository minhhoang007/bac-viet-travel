Ha Long Bay is a **UNESCO World Heritage Site** with nearly 2,000 limestone islands. Staying overnight is the best way to see the bay at sunset and sunrise, after the day boats have gone.

<Callout>Best season: March–May and September–November. Storms are possible in July–August; itineraries follow the bay authority's notices.</Callout>

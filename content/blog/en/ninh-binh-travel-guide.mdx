Ninh Binh, "Ha Long Bay on land", is about 2 hours from Hanoi. The **Trang An Landscape Complex** is a UNESCO mixed World Heritage Site.

## Trang An or Tam Coc?
- **Trang An:** a 2–3 hour boat ride through many caves and temples, wilder scenery.
- **Tam Coc:** about 1.5 hours, best when the rice turns gold (late May to early June).

## Climbing Mua Cave
About 500 stone steps. Go early in the morning or late in the afternoon, bring water and shoes with good grip.

<Callout>The Tam Coc rice is usually golden in late May. It is the busiest time of year, so book early.</Callout>

## Suggested itineraries
- **1 day:** Hoa Lu, Trang An, Mua Cave.
- **2 days:** add Bai Dinh pagoda, cycling in Tam Coc and a homestay night.

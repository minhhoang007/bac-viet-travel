Ha Long Bay is about 2.5 hours from Hanoi on the expressway and is a **UNESCO World Heritage Site** with nearly 2,000 limestone islands.

## Best time to visit
- **March to May:** mild, little rain, clear water.
- **September to November:** sunny, great for kayaking.
- **July and August:** storm season; boats may be stopped by the bay authority.

## Day trip or overnight?
| | Day trip | Overnight cruise |
|---|---|---|
| Time on the bay | About 4 hours | Almost 24 hours |
| Sunset and sunrise | No | Yes |
| Best for | Short on time | The full experience |

<Callout>If you can, stay overnight: the bay is at its best in the evening and early morning, after the day boats have left.</Callout>

## Don't miss
1. **Sung Sot Cave:** one of the largest caves in the bay.
2. **Ti Top Island:** about 400 steps to a panoramic view.
3. **Kayaking through Luon Cave:** into a lagoon enclosed by cliffs.

## What to pack
Sunscreen, a hat, water shoes and a light jacket for evenings on deck.

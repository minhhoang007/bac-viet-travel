Sapa is about 5.5 hours from Hanoi on the expressway. Trekking the Muong Hoa valley is the best way to see the rice terraces and villages.

## Best season
- **September to early October:** golden rice.
- **May–June:** water season, terraces like mirrors.
- **December–January:** cold, sometimes frost on Fansipan.

## The Lao Chai – Ta Van trail
About 12 km, mostly dirt paths through the fields with a few steep sections. Anyone in normal health can do it; no climbing experience needed.

<Callout tone="warning">Trails are slippery after rain. Bring trainers with good grip, a rain jacket and a warm layer, even in summer.</Callout>

## Is Fansipan worth it?
The cable car takes you near the 3,143 m summit in about 15 minutes. Go in the morning, when there are fewer clouds.
